SMP Server Rules

  • No Griefing: Do not destroy or steal other players' builds.
  • No Cheating: Hacks, mods, or exploits are strictly prohibited.
  • Respect Everyone: No abusive or toxic behavior.
  • No Spamming: Avoid spamming chat or commands.
  • Fair Play: Maintain a fun and balanced gameplay environment.
  • Follow Admins: Respect server admins and their decisions.
